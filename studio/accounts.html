Accounts to open.

Forma runs on a handful of accounts, each in your studio's name. This is the list, what each is for, and what it costs.

All resources

For studio owners. Updated November 2026.

Three rules first

  • Use a studio email address, such as info@yourstudio.com, not a personal one. The accounts should belong to the business.
  • Turn on 2-step verification on every account, and on the email inbox itself. Sign-in codes go to your inbox, so your inbox is your key.
  • Never send anyone a password. Each service lets you invite a team member. Invite me that way, and remove me whenever you like. Keep your own logins in a password manager.

Required

  • Hosting: Render

    Runs Forma and stores your studio's data.

    You do: create the account with your studio email and card, turn on 2-step login, choose the Pro workspace, invite me as a team member. Cost: about $75–80 a month.

  • Card payments: Stripe

    Takes payments and pays out to your bank. If you already have a Stripe account for the studio, we use it.

    You do: create the account in the business's legal name, add your bank account, finish Stripe's verification, invite me as a team member. Cost: Stripe's standard card rate; no monthly fee.

  • Email delivery: Resend

    Sends confirmations, reminders, receipts and sign-in codes from your studio's address.

    You do: create the account and invite me. I give you a few records to add to your domain so email arrives from your address. Cost: about $20 a month.

  • Your domain

    Your booking pages live at an address like book.yourstudio.com.

    You do: find the login for wherever your domain is managed (GoDaddy, Squarespace, Wix, Google, Namecheap). I tell you exactly which records to add, or you invite me to add them. Cost: nothing new.

Recommended

  • Google sign-in

    Lets clients and staff tap "Continue with Google" instead of waiting for an emailed code.

    You do: create a Google Cloud project with your studio's Google account and invite me. Google needs a privacy policy published on your website. Cost: free.

  • AI key: Anthropic

    Powers the assistant and AI-written reports. Skip it if you'd rather not use AI; everything else works without it.

    You do: create the account, add a card, and set a monthly spending cap. I connect the key. Cost: about $5–10 a month for a typical studio.

Optional

  • Your marketing tool

    Keep what you have. Forma connects to Klaviyo, Constant Contact, Mailchimp and ActiveCampaign and keeps your list and tags current.

    You do: invite me, or create an access key when I ask. Cost: what you pay today.

  • A password manager

    One safe place for every login above, controlled by you. 1Password and Bitwarden both work well.

Also have ready

  • Your logo and one brand color.
  • Links to your terms, privacy policy and text-message terms, if you send texts.
  • Your waiver wording.
  • The end date and notice period of your current software contract.

If I disappear: every account above is yours, so your studio keeps running, and any developer can take over from the same logins.
