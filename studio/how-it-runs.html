How Forma runs and what it costs.

Forma is software you own a copy of. It runs on a few accounts in your studio's name, and you pay those providers directly.

All resources

For studio owners. Updated November 2026.

The short version

The software is free. Running it costs about $100 a month, paid to the companies that host it, not to me. Setup is a one-time fee if you want me to do it.

  • No license fee, no per-member fee, no contract.
  • Every account is in your studio's name. Hosting, payments, email. If I disappeared tomorrow, your studio would keep running.
  • Your clients book on your own web address, for example book.yourstudio.com, in your colors.

What it runs on

Forma is the studio software: schedule, clients, bookings, selling, reports. For hosting, card payments, email and AI it connects to services you choose and own.

There is no monthly fee to Forma. Once your studio is set up, you own it and run it.

What "you own it" means in practice

  • You hold the logins. Each account is opened with your studio's email. I work as an invited team member, and you can remove me at any time.
  • Your data stays yours. Your client list and history live in your own hosting account, never pooled with another studio's.
  • Any developer can maintain it. Forma is open source under the AGPL license, so you are never tied to me.
  • Nobody can raise the price on you. There is no price to raise.

How to compare it with what you pay now

Compare the $100 with your current studio software bill and its add-ons (branded app fees, extra locations, reporting tiers). Leave out the tools you will keep either way, like your marketing platform. If your current software bundles marketing, add the cost of a marketing tool to Forma's side so the comparison is fair.
