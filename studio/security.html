Where Forma runs

Your own account, in your studio's name.

Forma runs on Render, a hosting company that runs websites and apps for businesses of every size. Instead of a computer sitting in your back office, Forma runs in Render's data centers in the U.S., and Render keeps the machines, updates and network running.

Each studio gets its own Render account, in the studio's name. Forma sets it up and hands it over. You own it, Render bills you directly, and your client data is never pooled with another studio's. If you ever leave Forma, everything stays with you.

Think of a bank building. Render runs the building. Forma builds your safe-deposit box inside it, fits a strong lock, and hands you the key. The box, and everything in it, is yours.

Protection

Eight ways your data is protected.

1

Locked in transit

Everything between a browser and Forma is encrypted: the padlock in the address bar.

2

A database with no front door

Only your Forma app can reach your database, over a private connection. The stored data and its backups are encrypted.

3

No passwords to steal

Clients and staff sign in with Google or a one-time code sent to their email. Codes expire in 15 minutes and stop working after 5 wrong tries.

4

The right keys for the right people

Instructors see what instructors need. Money, exports and settings are for managers and admins only.

5

A bouncer at the door

Render absorbs attack floods automatically. Forma limits how many sign-in codes anyone can request, how many guesses they get, and how heavily the assistant can be used.

6

An AI assistant that asks first

It can't change anything until a staff member clicks Approve, and every action is logged. It runs on a paid AI plan that does not use your data to train AI.

7

No card numbers, ever

Cards are stored by Stripe, in your studio's own Stripe account. Forma never sees or saves full card numbers.

8

Rewind any mistake

Your database can be rolled back to any point in the last 7 days, so a bad day is undone instead of lost.

Your part

Three things only you can do.

Forma builds the lock. These three habits keep the key in the right hands.

  1. Turn on 2-step verification for your email and your Render account.Sign-in codes go to your inbox, so your inbox is your key.
  2. Give each staff member the lowest role they need.An instructor doesn't need refunds or settings.
  3. Remove staff access the day someone leaves.It takes one click in Settings.

If an alert arrives: forward it to your developer, paste it into an AI assistant, or send it to Forma for a paid review.
