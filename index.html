Selected Work

Recent Projects

01
Operational Consulting Ownership Transition Social Media Setup

Yoga Studio, Alpharetta, GA

A local yoga studio going through a change of ownership. The new owner inherited a business she believed in but had no documentation for — accounts she didn't have access to, a website on a platform nobody remembered choosing, and social media assets scattered across two people's logins.

What I Did

  • Mapped the full operational stack the previous owner had been running
  • Transferred social media accounts and admin access to the new owner
  • Identified the website platform and set up access
  • Stood up a clean social media operation (Meta Business Suite, posting workflow, scheduling)
  • Consulted through the operational transition — on-site, in the studio
Outcome The handoff between owners became an actual handoff, not a guessing game. The new owner runs the studio without relying on the former owner for basic operational questions.
02
AI Tools & Automation Operational Upgrade Custom Build

Little Sponges

Natalia at Little Sponges came in with a messy HubSpot and a lot of questions about AI. What started as a CRM audit turned into a full operational upgrade.

What I Did

  • Audited HubSpot and delivered a prioritized recommendations presentation
  • Migrated her daily workflow from ChatGPT to Claude, including Claude Code for agentic tasks
  • Set up a modern AI tool stack — ElevenLabs for voice, video generation tools, agentic workflow design
  • Designed and built a custom AI-powered Spanish learning app to demonstrate what's possible for her business
Outcome Natalia is now running her AI-enabled operation herself. The consulting kick-started her technical fluency — which was the real goal.
